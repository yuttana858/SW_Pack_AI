# Containerboard paper grades, nomenclature and paper test methods

Scope: the papers that make up corrugated board (liners and fluting/medium), how they are named in Europe and North America, the laboratory tests used to grade them, and published typical values. Grade *limits* below are from the Cepi ContainerBoard list [S8] (reference). All mill property values are **industry-typical** (taken from individual mill data sheets) — *replace with supplier/house data in `company/`*.

The SCA, Saica, Palm, Burgo, Billerud and Schoeller & Hoesch sheets ([S10]–[S19]) could not be re-opened (hosts not reachable from the research environment); their values are carried over from wave 1 extracts. ## Liner and fluting families (overview)

Containerboard splits into **liners** (the flat facings) and **fluting / corrugating medium** (the wavy middle). Fibre origin is the main grade divider [S8][S9]:

| Family | Main fibre | Typical role |
|---|---|---|
| Kraftliner (brown, unbleached) | Predominantly primary kraft pulp [S8][S9] | Outer/inner liner where strength and wet performance matter |
| White-top / fully white kraftliner | Predominantly primary kraft pulp, white top ply (or fully white) [S8] | Printed outer liner |
| White mottled kraftliner | Predominantly primary kraft pulp; brightness ≥ 50 % only [S8] | Lower-cost print liner |
| Brown kraft top liner | Recycled base with a top layer predominantly of virgin fibre [S8] | Recycled liner with kraft surface |
| Testliner (TL1–TL4), brown | Predominantly recycled fibre [S8][S9] | General-purpose liners |
| White-top testliner (grades A/B/C, coated) | Recycled base with white-fibre top [S8][S23] | Printed outer liner, recycled |
| Mottled testliner | Recycled base with uneven white-fibre top [S8] | Low-cost print liner |
| Semichemical fluting (SC1, SC2) | Predominantly semichemical primary fibre; SC1 generally > 80 % semichemical fibre [S8]; NSSC hardwood in North America [S26] | High-performance fluting |
| Recycled fluting ("Wellenstoff", Medium 1/2, HP 2/3) | Predominantly recycled fibre, ≥ 100 g/m² [S8] | Standard and high-performance fluting |
| Light-weight recycled medium (LWM) | Predominantly recycled fibre, < 100 g/m² [S8] | Fine flutes, light boards |

The Cepi ContainerBoard "European list of corrugated base papers" (5th edition, 22 September 2015) is the reference grade classification in Europe [S8]; the 2021 FEFCO/Cepi LCA database still cites this edition [S40]. Key rules [S8]:

- Liners are graded on **burst index OR SCT-CD index** (either criterion qualifies); flutings on CMT30 index and/or SCT-CD (or CCT) index.
- All tabulated values are **guaranteed minimum (or maximum) values, never nominal** values.
- Burst may be tested to ISO 2758 or ISO 2759; the list gives separate limits for each. Its units table assigns ISO 2758 + ISO 2759 above 350 kPa and ISO 2758 below 350 kPa.
- "Guaranteed value" = lowest customer-reel mean; "typical value" = long-term (≥ 6 months) production average; characteristics are met if 97.5 % of values are not below the guaranteed value.
- Grammage tolerance: ± 3 % of agreed value up to 200 g/m², ± 4 % above 200 g/m².
- Grade numbers not allocated by Cepi must not be used.

Indices are property ÷ grammage: burst index in kPa·m²/g; SCT-CD index and CCT index in N·m/g (= kN·m/kg); CMT30 index in N·m²/g.

Edition check: a later (sixth) revision of the list was reported in trade press and the association has since been renamed Containerboard Europe; that revision was not retrieved — **VERIFY** limits against the current edition before quoting them as normative.

Notes:
- Testliner grades are contiguous: the minimum of one grade is the maximum of the next lower grade, and a paper that exceeds either the burst or the SCT limit of the next grade is automatically classified in that higher grade. Below the TL4 limits a paper may not be called "testliner" [S8].
- The "≥ 1.7 or ≥ 13 / ≥ 1.5 or ≥ 12 / ≥ 1.3 or ≥ 11" threshold pairs seen in earlier extracts belong to **white-top testliner grades A / B / C**, not to TL1–TL3 [S8].
- TL1–TL3 can be supplied unsized, sized (Cobb60 typically 25–45 g/m²) or "special sized" (used to meet the UN dangerous-goods requirements); the suffix is coded in the grade number [S8].
- Brightness is ISO 2470-1 (C/2°, UV adjusted to ISO IR3); roughness Bendtsen ISO 8791-2 (ml/min), PPS ISO 8791-4 [S8].

Worked example (derived from [S8]): minimum burst for a 120 g/m² TL2 = 2.2 × 120 = 264 kPa (ISO 2758) or 2.5 × 120 = 300 kPa (ISO 2759); minimum SCT-CD = 15.5 × 120 / 1000 = 1.86 kN/m.

## Cepi grade limits — flutings (reference)

| Grade (Cepi no.) | CMT30 index (N·m²/g) | SCT-CD index (N·m/g) | CCT30 index (N·m/g) | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Semichemical 1 (40) | ≥ 2.2 | ≥ 21.0 | ≥ 20.0 | CMT **and** either CCT or SCT |
| Semichemical 2 (46) | > 1.9 | > 17.0 | > 16.0 | CMT **and** either CCT or SCT |
| Medium high performance 2 (45) | ≥ 2.0 | ≥ 19.0 | — | CMT **or** SCT |
| Medium high performance 3 (44) | ≥ 1.8 | ≥ 17.0 | — | CMT **or** SCT |
| Medium 1 (41) | ≥ 1.6 | ≥ 15.0 | — | CMT **or** SCT |
| Medium 2 (43) | ≥ 1.3 | ≥ 13.5 | — | CMT **or** SCT |

Light-weight medium (LWM, Cepi no. 60, < 100 g/m²) is specified in absolute values: 95 g/m² → SCT-CD ≥ 1.45 kN/m; 90 → ≥ 1.35; 85 → ≥ 1.30; 80 → ≥ 1.15; 75 → ≥ 1.00 kN/m; CMT30 values (135 / 125 / 110 / 95 / 90 N) are indicative only [S8].

- Recycled fluting is ≥ 100 g/m² by definition; exceeding either the CMT30 or the SCT-CD limit moves a medium into the next higher grade [S8].
- The correlation between CCT and SCT-CD is different for semichemical fibre than for other fibres [S8], so do not convert CCT↔SCT with recycled-fluting correlations.

## Cepi moisture windows (terms of guarantee)

| Paper | Agreed reel-average moisture | Default if not stated | CD peak-to-peak around mean (15 cm box) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kraftliner | 6.5–9.5 % | 8.0 % | ± 1.5 %-units |
| Testliner and other recycled liners | 6.0–9.0 % | 7.5 % | ± 1.5 %-units |
| Semichemical fluting | 7.5–11 % | 9.0 % | ± 2 %-units |
| Recycled fluting / medium | 6.5–9.5 % | 8.0 % | ± 2 %-units |

Adjacent 15 cm measuring boxes may differ by at most 2.8 %-units for all grades [S8]. Fibre/TSO angle should be within about ± 5° to avoid paper-related twist warp [S8].

## Kraftliner (unbleached brown)

- Made predominantly from primary kraft pulp [S8][S9]; grammages offered by mills run from about 100 to 275 g/m² (e.g. 100–200 g/m² [S10]; 115–275 g/m² [S34]).
- Published values at nominal 125 g/m² (industry-typical, three mills):

| SCT-CD | 2.55 / ≥ 2.20 kN/m | 2.6 kN/m | 2.8 / ≥ 2.4 kN/m |
| Cobb 60 | 26 / ≤ 32 g/m² | 30 g/m² | 30 / ≤ 40 g/m² |
| Scott bond | 320 / ≥ 220 J/m² (TAPPI T 569) | — | — |
| Moisture basis | 8.0 ± 1 %; tests at 23 °C / 50 % RH | not stated | 9.5 % (nominal) |

**Burst conflict resolved (with one open check):** two independent mill sheets agree on ≈ 560–570 kPa at 125 g/m² [S10][S34] (burst index ≈ 4.5 kPa·m²/g). The Saica figures (400 / 350 kPa → index 3.2 / 2.8) are **below the Cepi kraftliner minimum** of 3.5 (ISO 2758) / 3.6 (ISO 2759), i.e. 437.5 / 450 kPa at 125 g/m² [S8] (derived). They therefore cannot be the burst of a Cepi-grade-00 kraftliner as written; the likeliest explanations are a misread column or a different product. ## RCT from SCT (derived estimates)

Measured North American RCT values per grade were **not retrieved** (US mill hosts unreachable in wave 2). DS Smith publishes the Lorentzen & Wettre general regression between ring crush and SCT [S37]:

**Equation:** RCT_CD = 120 × SCT_CD − 97, or approximately RCT_CD = 95 × SCT_CD

| Variable | Meaning | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| RCT_CD | ring crush force, cross direction | N (on the standard 152.4 mm / 6 in ring strip) |
| SCT_CD | short-span compression, cross direction | kN/m |

- **Validity:** a linear regression over papers of different basis weights; it is specific to each paper machine and should be agreed between producer and user from dedicated tests [S37]. Treat results as ± 20 % estimates.
- **Unit conversion:** RCT (lbf/6 in) × 0.02919 = kN/m [S38]; equivalently RCT (N) ÷ 152.4 mm = kN/m and RCT (N) ÷ 4.448 = lbf (derived).
- **Worked example (SI / US):** DS Smith kraftliner 125 g/m², SCT-CD 2.55 kN/m [S34] → RCT ≈ 120 × 2.55 − 97 ≈ 209 N (≈ 1.37 kN/m, ≈ 47 lbf/6 in) or 95 × 2.55 ≈ 242 N (≈ 1.59 kN/m, ≈ 54 lbf/6 in) (derived).

| Paper (typical SCT-CD [S34]) | RCT_CD, 120·SCT − 97 | RCT_CD, 95·SCT |
|---|---|---|
| Kraftliner 125 g/m² (2.55 kN/m) | ≈ 209 N ≈ 47 lbf/6 in | ≈ 242 N ≈ 54 lbf/6 in |
| Kraftliner 186 g/m² (3.85 kN/m) | ≈ 365 N ≈ 82 lbf/6 in | ≈ 366 N ≈ 82 lbf/6 in |
| Liner 1 (TL1) 120 g/m² (2.35 kN/m) | ≈ 185 N ≈ 42 lbf/6 in | ≈ 223 N ≈ 50 lbf/6 in |
| Liner 3 (TL3) 120 g/m² (2.10 kN/m) | ≈ 155 N ≈ 35 lbf/6 in | ≈ 200 N ≈ 45 lbf/6 in |
| Medium Plus 100 g/m² (1.85 kN/m) | ≈ 125 N ≈ 28 lbf/6 in | ≈ 176 N ≈ 40 lbf/6 in |
| Medium HP Plus 120 g/m² (2.50 kN/m) | ≈ 203 N ≈ 46 lbf/6 in | ≈ 238 N ≈ 53 lbf/6 in |

All values in this table are **derived** (arithmetic from [S34][S37]); the two equations diverge most at low SCT. **Limits:** RCT on light papers fails by buckling and depends on caliper and specimen holder, which is one reason European grading uses SCT [S37]. *Replace with supplier/house data in `company/`.*

## European paper and board naming codes

- Combined board is written outer liner / inner liner with grammage and a paper-type letter, followed by the flute profile. DS Smith examples: **125K/125T EB** = 125 g/m² kraft outside (on the E flute) and 125 g/m² test inside (on the B flute); **190Y/180W E** = clay-coated liner outside, white-top kraft inside [S36]. The liner named first is always the outside [S36]. A similar form **125K/B/125T** is used by UK converters [S32][S33].
- In double-wall flute codes the first letter is the outer flute (EB = E outside, B inside) [S36]. A centre liner, when it must be specified, is written between the two outer/inner liners; DS Smith supplies standard fluting unless an enhanced fluting (127 / 150 / 175 g/m²) is specified [S36].
- Liner reference letters published by one converter (DS Smith Sheetfeeding) [S35]:

| Letter | Liner |
|---|---|
| K | Brown kraftliner |
| T | Fully recycled liner (TL3) |
| L | Higher-performance recycled liner (TL2) |
| W | White-top kraftliner (WTK) |
| D | White-top recycled liner (WTT) |
| M | Mottled kraftliner |
| FBK | Fully bleached kraftliner |
| Y | Clay-coated liner |
| C | Waste-based liner |

- K = kraft and T = test are also confirmed by UK converter guides [S32][S33]. Other letters are **house conventions, not a standard** — the same letter can differ between converters, and B or C can mean a flute profile. Letters for fluting papers (e.g. S semichemical, F or M for recycled fluting) were not found in a retrievable source — **VERIFY** against your converter's grade key.
- When a fluting grade is written instead of the flute letter (e.g. "125K/112F/125K"), the middle term is the fluting grammage and paper type and the flute profile must be stated separately.

## Paper test methods and what they predict

Summaries only — consult the standard for procedure. Edition status changes; check the ISO TC 6 catalogue [S7] and TAPPI store before citing an edition. Cross-references below follow the FEFCO world-standards comparison [S39] and the Cepi units table [S8].

| Test | ISO | TAPPI | Measures | Predicts / used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SCT (short-span compression) | ISO 9895 [S8][S39] | T 826 (identical procedure per [S39]; om:2021 current per listing [S4]) | In-plane compressive strength of paper over a very short span, MD and CD [S1] | Good estimate of combined-board ECT; main liner/fluting grade criterion in Europe [S1][S8] |
| RCT (ring crush) | ISO 12192 [S39] | T 822 rigid support (0.28–0.61 mm board) [S2]; T 818 flexible beam (0.28–0.51 mm) [S3] | Edgewise compression of a ring-shaped strip | North American liner/medium compression grade; ECT/BCT estimates; can be estimated from SCT [S37] |
| CMT (Concora medium test) | ISO 7263 [S8] | T 809 [S5][S6] | Flat crush of laboratory-fluted medium | Fluting grade criterion in Europe [S8]; flat crush and runnability |
| CCT (corrugated crush test) | ISO 16945 [S17]; Cepi list cites SCAN P42 [S8] | — | Edgewise compression of laboratory-fluted medium | Alternative to SCT for semichemical fluting; correlation differs for semichemical [S8] |
| Burst (paper/liner) | ISO 2758; ISO 2759 also permitted for liners (Cepi gives separate limits) [S8] | T 807 (linerboard) [S1][S5] | Hydraulic bursting pressure | Liner toughness; burst-index grade criterion in Europe [S8] |
| Burst (combined board) | ISO 2759 | T 810 [S30] | Bursting pressure of corrugated/solid fibreboard | Mullen-based North American board grades |
| Cobb water absorption | ISO 535 [S8] | T 441 [S31] | Water absorbed in a set time (e.g. 60 s), g/m² | Glue-line / printing / humidity behaviour; sizing level |
| Tensile / tensile stiffness | ISO 1924-2; Cepi recommends ISO 1924-3 for tensile stiffness [S8] | T 494 [S1] | Tensile strength, stretch, TEA, stiffness | Runnability; Cepi recommends mills publish tensile stiffness for box modelling [S8] |
| Moisture content | ISO 287 (oven drying) [S8] | T 412 (identical) [S1][S39] | Mass loss on drying, % | Corrugator warp, conditioning; property values are moisture-dependent |
| Brightness | ISO 2470-1 (C/2°); ISO 5631-1 colour recommended in addition [S8] | — | Blue reflectance factor R457 | White-liner grade criterion [S8] |
| Roughness | ISO 8791-2 (Bendtsen), ISO 8791-4 (PPS) [S8] | — | Surface roughness | White/coated-liner grade criterion [S8] |
| Air permeance (porosity) | ISO 5636-5 (Gurley) [S17] | — | Air resistance in seconds | Vacuum handling, adhesive penetration |

Mill sheets state test conditions such as 23 °C / 50 % RH [S34] and moisture windows such as 6.5–9 % [S15]; property values are often quoted at a reference moisture basis (e.g. 7.5 % [S12]), so always compare at the same basis.

## How to use these values

1. Treat every mill number here as a starting estimate, never a specification; Cepi limits are guaranteed minima, mill "typical" values are long-term averages [S8].
2. Use SCT-CD (Europe) or RCT (North America) of each ply to estimate ECT — see `performance/` files.
3. Record the actual supplier-certified values in `company/materials/` using `materials/material-record-schema.md`; those override this file.
