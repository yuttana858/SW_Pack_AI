# Packaging regulations, recyclability and sustainability claims

Regulatory status changes quickly. Everything here reflects sources consulted on **2026-11-09**. Research note: sources were reached through search-engine summaries only (the session could not open EUR-Lex or the state agency sites), and the search budget ran out before the FBA, CEPI, 4evergreen, ISO, CEN, FSC/PEFC/SFI, FTC and LCA sources could be consulted. Sections marked **SOURCE PENDING** carry no numbers and must be completed from primary sources. For anything normative, point engineers to the legal text (EUR-Lex, state statute or rule) and to company legal or compliance staff. Do not give legal advice.

## EU PPWR — Regulation (EU) 2025/40: status and key dates

- Published in the Official Journal on 22 January 2025, in force 11 February 2025, and **applies from 12 August 2026** [S1][S2][S3].
- Many obligations run on their own dates, and some depend on Commission delegated or implementing acts. The later of a fixed date and "N months after the act" is common [S16]. Confirm every date against EUR-Lex.

| Topic (Article, per secondary sources) | Date | Status note |
|---|---|---|
| General application | 12 Aug 2026 | Firm [S1][S2] |
| EPR information on pack only via QR code or similar digital marking (Art. 12(9)) | 12 Feb 2027 | Per [S11] (VERIFY) |
| Sales packaging empty space "minimum necessary" (Art. 24(4)) | 12 Feb 2028 | Per [S6][S7] |
| Harmonised sorting label (Art. 12(1)) | Later of 12 Aug 2028 or 24 months after the implementing act | The act was due 12 Aug 2026 and the deadline was missed [S10][S11]. One report points to adoption in Q2 2027, implying around mid-2029 (planning record, not law) [S11]. VERIFY |
| Recyclability grade C minimum (Art. 6) | Later of 1 Jan 2030 or 24 months after the design-for-recycling delegated acts | Conditional [S4][S5] |
| Empty space ratio ≤ 50 % for grouped, transport and e-commerce packaging (Art. 24) | Later of 1 Jan 2030 or 3 years after the calculation-method implementing act | Conditional [S6][S7] |
| Reuse targets for transport packaging (Art. 29) | 1 Jan 2030 (binding); 2040 (endeavour) | [S8][S9] |
| Only grades A and B allowed (Art. 6) | 1 Jan 2038 | [S4][S5] |

## EU PPWR — recyclability performance grades

Article 6 and Annex II set performance grades by the share of the packaging unit (by weight) that is recyclable, as assessed under design-for-recycling criteria to be set by delegated act [S4][S5]:

| Grade | Recyclability (by weight) | Market access |
|---|---|---|
| A | ≥ 95 % | Allowed |
| B | ≥ 80 % | Allowed |
| C | ≥ 70 % | Allowed from 2030; banned from 1 Jan 2038 |
| Below C | < 70 % | Not allowed from 2030 (conditional date) |

Thresholds from secondary commentary [S4][S5]. **VERIFY** against Annex II of the Regulation and the delegated acts. Until those acts are adopted, no official A/B/C grading can be claimed [S4]. Material-specific criteria for paper and board will come in the delegated acts. Industry protocols (CEPI, 4evergreen) are expected to inform them: **SOURCE PENDING**.

## EU PPWR — empty space ratio (Article 24) and minimisation (Article 10)

- Economic operators who fill grouped, transport or e-commerce packaging must keep the **empty space ratio at or below 50 %**. Filling materials (paper, air cushions, bubble wrap, foam) count as empty space [S6][S7].
- The Commission sets the calculation method by implementing act. The 50 % limit applies from the later of 1 January 2030 or 3 years after that act [S6][S7].
- **Exemptions**: sales packaging also used as e-commerce packaging (Art. 24(5)), which still has to meet the Art. 10 minimisation duty; and reusable packaging in a re-use system [S6][S7].
- Article 10 requires packaging weight and volume to be the minimum needed for function (protection, handling, information) [S6].

### Empty space ratio working formula (pending the official method)

```
ESR = (V_pack − V_product) / V_pack × 100 %
```

- `V_pack`: volume of the outer (grouped, transport or e-commerce) packaging (L) [in³]. Inside or outside basis is set by the implementing act. Until then state which you used (VERIFY).
- `V_product`: volume of the sales units or products contained (L) [in³]. Filling material counts as empty space, so it is **not** included [S6][S7].

Worked example, SI: box inside 400 × 300 × 200 mm (24.0 L) holding one 300 × 200 × 120 mm product (7.2 L): ESR = (24.0 − 7.2)/24.0 = **70.0 %**, above the limit. Right-sized to 310 × 210 × 130 mm (8.463 L): ESR = **14.9 %**.
Worked example, US: box inside 16 × 12 × 8 in (1536 in³ = 25.17 L) holding a 12 × 8 × 5 in product (480 in³): ESR = **68.75 %**. Right-sized to 12.5 × 8.5 × 5.5 in (584.4 in³): ESR = **17.9 %**.

Limits: this is a working definition. Irregular products, the treatment of the product's own packaging, and the inside-versus-outside basis await the implementing act. Derived.

## EU PPWR — reuse targets and labelling

- **Reuse (Art. 29)**: from 1 January 2030, at least 40 % of transport packaging (including e-commerce) must be reusable within a re-use system. For 2040 the figure is 70 %, framed as "shall endeavour" [S8][S9]. Secondary sources report that **cardboard boxes are excluded** from the transport and grouped packaging reuse targets (Art. 29(4)–(5)) [S8]. Pallet wrapping and straps have specific exemptions [S9]. Sources disagree on several sub-targets, so **VERIFY** in the legal text.
- **Labelling (Art. 12)**: a harmonised material-composition sorting label is required from the later of 12 August 2028 or 24 months after the implementing act. That act missed its 12 August 2026 deadline [S10][S11]. Packaging made or imported before the labelling date may be sold for three more years [S11] (VERIFY). National schemes (for example France's Triman) stay in force meanwhile [S11]. Advice from the sources: do not commission harmonised-label artwork until the act is adopted [S11].

Use for design: EPR fees are usually eco-modulated by material and recyclability, so the design-for-recycling checklist below lowers cost as well as compliance risk.

## Repulpability, wax and water-resistant coatings — SOURCE PENDING

- Wax-impregnated or wax-cascaded corrugated is traditionally excluded from standard old corrugated container (OCC) recycling streams. The Fibre Box Association (FBA) **Voluntary Standard for Repulping and Recycling Corrugated Fiberboard Treated to Improve its Performance in the Presence of Water and Water Vapor** sets a lab protocol for repulpability and recyclability of treated boxes. Certified boxes may carry the FBA recyclable mark. **SOURCE PENDING**: get the current protocol, pass criteria and certified-product list from the FBA before quoting it.
- Barrier coatings, plastic laminates, heavy UV varnishes, metallised films, wet-strength resins and hot-melt adhesives can all affect repulping yield and stickies. Ask the coating supplier for repulpability test data from a recognised protocol (FBA in the US; the CEPI harmonised method or 4evergreen protocol in Europe). **SOURCE PENDING**.
- Water-based inkjet prints: a German test body (PTS) reported that HP water-based digital prints introduced no optically disturbing inhomogeneities in recycled pulp [S17].

## Recyclability protocols and standards — SOURCE PENDING

Name the protocol when making any recyclability statement. Summaries are to be completed from primary sources:

- **CEPI / 4evergreen**: European paper-industry laboratory test method and recyclability evaluation protocol for fibre-based packaging. SOURCE PENDING.
- **ISO 18601 to ISO 18606**: the ISO family on packaging and the environment (general requirements, source reduction, reuse, material recycling, energy recovery, organic recycling). SOURCE PENDING: confirm titles and current editions.
- **EN 13432**: EU harmonised standard for packaging recoverable through composting and biodegradation. Pass criteria (disintegration, biodegradation, ecotoxicity, heavy metals) must be quoted from the standard. SOURCE PENDING.

## Forest certification and chain of custody — SOURCE PENDING

- **FSC**, **PEFC** and **SFI** certify forest management and, through chain-of-custody (CoC) certification, the tracking of certified fibre through each company that takes ownership (mill, converter, printer).
- An on-pack logo needs a valid CoC certificate at every step and trademark approval from the scheme. Claim type (100 %, Mix, Recycled and their equivalents) follows each scheme's rules. SOURCE PENDING: cite the scheme standards before stating thresholds.
- The assistant must never state that a product is certified unless the CoC certificate number comes from `company/` records.

## Recycled content and environmental claims (FTC Green Guides) — SOURCE PENDING

- In the US, environmental marketing claims ("recyclable", "recycled content", "compostable") fall under the FTC *Guides for the Use of Environmental Marketing Claims* (16 CFR Part 260). A revision has been under review; check its current status. SOURCE PENDING.
- Engineering practice: give recycled content as a percentage by weight with its basis (pre-consumer or post-consumer) and evidence from the mill. Qualify "recyclable" where collection access is limited. Do not make unqualified general claims ("eco-friendly").
- In the EU, PPWR labelling and the EU rules on green claims also apply. SOURCE PENDING.

## Carbon footprint (LCA) headline numbers — SOURCE PENDING

- The **FEFCO** (European corrugated LCA database, with CEPI) and **FBA** (US corrugated industry LCA) publish cradle-to-grave figures per tonne of corrugated product. No figures are given here until the current report is consulted: **SOURCE PENDING**. When quoting, give the functional unit, system boundary, year of data and geography.
- Rules for comparison: compare LCAs only with the same functional unit and boundary. Right-sizing (less board and less transport cube) usually outweighs small differences between material grades. Show that with your own LCA tool rather than generic numbers.

## Design-for-recycling checklist (corrugated and paperboard)

Engineering checklist, derived from the regulatory drivers above. Confirm the material criteria once the delegated acts and protocols are in hand.

1. **Mono-material fibre** where possible. Avoid laminated plastic films, metallised layers and non-separable plastic windows, or make them easy to separate.
2. **Coatings and wax**: use only treatments with repulpability evidence (FBA protocol or CEPI/4evergreen). Record the test report in `company/`.
3. **Adhesives and tapes**: prefer water-based or repulpable adhesives and paper tape. Minimise hot-melt.
4. **Inks and varnish**: water-based or low-migration systems. Avoid heavy UV coatings over whole surfaces unless repulpability is shown [S17].
5. **Right-size**: check the empty space ratio (≤ 50 % target under Art. 24) and minimise per Art. 10 [S6][S7].
6. **Remove unnecessary components**: double walls, inner sleeves, dunnage.
7. **Labelling**: plan space for the EU harmonised label once adopted, and avoid conflicting on-pack claims [S10][S11].
8. **Evidence**: keep a technical file (recyclability assessment, protocol and lab, material composition, recycled content evidence, certification CoC numbers).

## Right-sizing and void ratio formulas

```
Void ratio      VR  = 1 − V_product / V_inside
Cube efficiency CE  = V_product / V_inside   (= 1 − VR)
Board saving    ΔA% = (A_blank,old − A_blank,new) / A_blank,old × 100 %
```

- `V_product`: volume of the product or its bounding box (L) [in³]. State which.
- `V_inside`: inside volume of the shipper from **inside dimensions** L × W × D (L) [in³].
- `A_blank`: blank area of the box from the blank formulas in `dimensional/` (m²) [ft²].

VR on the inside basis matches the ESR worked example above. For the 400 × 300 × 200 mm box holding 7.2 L, VR = 70.0 %; after right-sizing, 14.9 % (SI). The US example gives 68.75 % → 17.9 %. Limits: bounding-box volume overstates the fill for irregular products. Cushioning needs (see `cushioning/`) set a lower limit on clearance, so do not right-size below the cushion thickness the fragility analysis requires. Derived.
