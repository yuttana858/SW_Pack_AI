# Packaging test standards index

## How to use this index

This index lists the test standards packaging engineers use most often for paper, board, corrugated containers and transport packages. For each standard it gives the number, a short title, the publisher, what it measures and when to use it. It is a pointer, not a substitute: the normative text (specimen size, rates, tolerances, report content) is in the standard itself, which must be bought from the publisher. Nothing here is copied from a standard.

**Verification status column.** Each row says how well its title and edition were checked on 2026-11-09:

- **Cited** — title and/or edition were confirmed from a publisher, catalogue or store listing, shown with an `[S#]` tag.
- **VERIFY** — the title, edition or status was **not** confirmed, or sources disagree; the row says which. Check the publisher catalogue before quoting it in a specification.

Editions change often (TAPPI reviews methods about every five years; ASTM reapproves on a five-year cycle). Always confirm the current edition before writing it into a specification.

Organisation abbreviations: TAPPI = Technical Association of the Pulp and Paper Industry; ISO = International Organization for Standardization; ASTM = ASTM International; EN = European Standard (CEN); ISTA = International Safe Transit Association.

## TAPPI test methods (paper, board, corrugated)

TAPPI edition suffixes: `om` = official method, `sp` = standard practice, `cm` = classical method. The number after the hyphen is the year of the edition.

## ISTA test procedures

ISTA groups its procedures in series. Descriptions below are paraphrased from ISTA's own test-procedures page [S25]:

- **1-Series, non-simulation integrity** (1A, 1B, 1C, 1D, 1E, 1G, 1H) — challenge the product–package combination; useful as screening tests, not designed to simulate transport.
- **2-Series, partial simulation** (2A, 2B, 2C) — 1-Series elements plus at least one 3-Series element such as atmospheric conditioning or mode-shaped random vibration; not intended to predict shipping performance.
- **3-Series, general simulation** (3A, 3B, 3E, 3F, 3H, 3K, 3L, 3M, 3N, 3P) — laboratory simulation of the damage-producing motions, forces, conditions and sequences of a shipment type; useful as a predictive tool.
- **4-Series, enhanced simulation** (4AB) — custom test plans tied to a user-defined distribution pattern, generated by web-based software.
- **6-Series, member performance** (6-AMAZON.COM, 6-SAMSCLUB) — protocols reflecting the supply chain of a large retailer or carrier. The FedEx 6-Series tests have been withdrawn (6-FEDEX-A in February 2024, 6-FEDEX-B in January 2025) [S25][S69].
- **7-Series, development** (7D, 7E) — compare relative performance of container designs; not intended to evaluate protection of the product.

Series 1 and 2 split at 150 lb (68 kg), with separate procedures for heavier items [S25]. ISTA 3A uses 70 kg (not 68 kg) as the equivalent of 150 lb because that is the usual parcel-carrier break point [S26]. Buy current procedures from ISTA; change notes are published on ISTA's procedure-changes page [S69].

## Choosing between TAPPI, ISO and ASTM routes

- **Material tests** (grammage, caliper, ECT, burst, Cobb): North American customers usually cite TAPPI; European and Asian customers usually cite ISO. ECT by T 811 (waxed edge), T 839 (clamp) and ISO 3037 (non-waxed) give different numbers for the same board, so always state the method with the value [S1][S14].
- **Container compression**: ASTM D642 allows fixed or swivel platens and is recognised as fulfilling ISO 12048; TAPPI T 804 does not recognise swivel-platen machines [S21]. Platen type changes the result, so record it.
- **Distribution testing**: ISTA procedures are prescriptive and widely accepted by retailers and carriers; ASTM D4169 is configurable (distribution cycle, assurance level) and is common in medical-device validation [S19][S28]. See `testing/distribution-testing.md`.
- **Conditioning**: TAPPI T 402 and ISO 187 for paper and board; ASTM D4332 and ISO 2233 for complete packages [S11][S18][S23].
