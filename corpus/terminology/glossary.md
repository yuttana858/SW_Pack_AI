# Packaging engineering glossary

> **How to use this file.** Definitions are working definitions in plain language, written for
> retrieval. They are not the normative definitions in ISO, TAPPI, ASTM, EN or FEFCO
> documents; for anything contractual, cite the standard. The main test-method numbers are
> given as pointers in the testing table (editions and status are in
> `testing/standards-index.md`); unit conversion factors and numeric limits are deliberately
> **not** given here: they live in `reference/` and `performance/` with their own citations. Where a definition
> rests on a consulted source it carries an [S#] tag; untagged definitions are general
> industry usage and were not checked against a published glossary during this review.
> Sources were consulted through search-engine excerpts on 2026-11-09.

## Box styles and box construction terms

Terms for naming corrugated box styles and their parts. Style codes are in
`styles/fefco-codes.md`.

|---|---|---|

| RSC (regular slotted container) | One-piece slotted box; all flaps the same length; outer flaps meet at the centre when closed [S1] | FEFCO 0201 [S1] |
| HSC (half slotted container) | An RSC without top flaps [S1] | FEFCO 0200 [S1] |
| OSC (overlap slotted container) | Slotted box whose outer flaps partially overlap [S1] | FEFCO 0202 |
| FOL (full overlap slotted container) | Slotted box whose outer flaps overlap the full width of the box [S1][S8] | FEFCO 0203 |

| Partition | Interlocking slotted strips forming cells inside a box | Divider, cell divider |

| Liner (fitment) | Sleeve or set of panels lining the inside walls of a box | Inner liner, sleeve (not containerboard liner) |
| Sleeve | Open-ended tube; slid over a tray or product | Band, slipcase |
| Panel | Any face of the box formed between scores (side, end, top, bottom) | Wall |
| Side panel | Longer wall of the box (on the length dimension) | Long panel |
| End panel | Shorter wall of the box (on the width dimension) | Short panel |
| Flap | Extension of a panel, hinged at a score, used to close the top or bottom | — |
| Outer flap | Flap on the length (side) panels; closes over the inner flaps | Major flap |
| Inner flap | Flap on the width (end) panels; closes first | Minor flap |
| Dust flap | Small flap that closes a gap at the end of a carton or folder; not structural | — |
| Tuck flap | Flap that tucks inside the box to close it | Tuck, tongue |
| Lock tab | Tab that engages a slot to hold a closure | Locking tab, lock |
| Manufacturer's joint | The seam where the two ends of the box blank are joined by the box maker, by glue, stitches (staples) or tape [S2][S9] | Mfr's joint, glue lap, side seam |
| User's joint | A joint closed by the user on their own equipment rather than by the box maker [S2] | — |
| Glue lap | The narrow flap on one end of the blank that is glued to the opposite end panel | Glue flap, glue tab |
| Stitched joint | Manufacturer's joint closed with wire staples | Stapled joint |
| Taped joint | Manufacturer's joint closed with tape | — |
| Inside dimensions (ID) | Dimensions of the inside of the closed box; FEFCO and FBA default for specifying boxes [S3][S4] | Internal dimensions |
| Outside dimensions (OD) | Overall external dimensions; used for pallet and cube planning | External dimensions |
| Manufacturing dimensions | Score-to-score dimensions on the blank, from which the box is made | Score dimensions, blank dimensions |
| L × W × D order | Box size order: Length then Width (the two opening dimensions, longer first) then Depth [S1] | FEFCO L × B × H [S3]; FEFCO 12th edition writes L × W × H [S12]; ECMA cartons use A × B × H measured at crease centres [S6] |
| Length (L) | Longer dimension of the opening [S3] | — |
| Width (W), Breadth (B) | Shorter dimension of the opening [S3] | FEFCO 12th edition says Width [S12]; the 11th edition and Recommendations 101 say Breadth [S11][S3] |

| Self-erecting | A box that forms itself (or nearly) when squared, e.g. crash-lock styles | Auto-erect |

| Display box | Box designed to present product at point of sale | POP / POS display |
| SIOC | Ships In Own Container: a product package strong enough to ship by parcel without an overbox (Amazon programme term) | Frustration-free packaging context |

## Board and box defects

Common defects seen on board, boxes and cartons.

| Term | Definition | Synonyms / notes |
|---|---|---|
| Washboarding | Visible flute lines on the liner surface, from a thin liner or a high-shrinkage glue line | Flute show-through |
| Warp | Board not flat (up, down, S or twist), usually from moisture imbalance between liners | Curl |
| Crush (board) | Flutes flattened by excessive pressure on the corrugator or converter | Crushed board, caliper loss |
| High / low flutes | Flutes formed with uneven height | Leaning flutes |
| Delamination | Liner separating from medium (weak bond) | Bond failure, zipper |
| Blister | Local unbonded area between liner and medium | — |
| Score cracking | Liner cracking along a score or crease | Crease cracking |
| Bulging | Box panels bowing outward under load or overfill | Bulge |
| Fishtail | See converting terms: skewed manufacturer's joint | — |
